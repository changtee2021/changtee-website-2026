# i18n glossary / คำศัพท์แปลภาษา

Use these terms consistently across UI messages and product overlays. Have a native speaker review before marketing launches in a market.

ใช้คำเหล่านี้ให้ตรงกันทุกหน้า และให้เจ้าของภาษาตรวจก่อนเปิดตลาดจริง

## Brand

- Brand name stays **ช่างตี๋ / Chang Tee** in every language. Do not translate it.
- LINE ID `@chang-tee`, phone numbers, and SKU/size codes (200×2 mm) are never translated.

## Chinese: Simplified vs Traditional

Traditional is written separately for Taiwan/Hong Kong readers. Do not just convert characters from Simplified:

- forklift: 叉车 (简) vs 堆高機 (繁)
- measuring: 上门测量 (简) vs 到府丈量 (繁)
- millimetre: 毫米 (简) vs 公釐 (繁)

## Style

- Short sentences, polite business tone, no slang.
- Burmese, Lao and Khmer: use native digits only where the existing text already does. Keep sizes such as `200×2` in Latin digits when they are product codes.
- Units: °C, mm, m stay as symbols.
