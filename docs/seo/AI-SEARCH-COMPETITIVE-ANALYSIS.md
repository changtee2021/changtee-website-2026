## Key Finding (Thailand, 2026)

Google states there are **no special requirements** to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode — they use the same Search systems. For **local transactional** queries (“ผ้าม่าน ใกล้ฉัน”, “ติดตั้งม่าน กรุงเทพ”), the **Map pack + GBP** still dominates over AI summaries.

For **informational** queries (“Dimout vs Blackout”, “ม่านม้วน vs มู่ลี่”), **AI Overviews cite editorial sources** (HomePro, Infinity, Proface) — opportunity for changtee-curtain.com blog URLs if answers are clearer and cited-worthy.

Sources: Google Search Central AI features docs; Inspira Digital Thailand GEO guides (2026).
